Pahnoawpa: F-Droid cha Privileged Extension he system "privileged-app" châta patyh awpa ta
root privileged-zy â byuh aw.

https://f-droid.org/packages/org.fdroid.fdroid cha dâh eihrâhpazy chvk pasainazy he 
hmâpa ta epalikeisiezy patyhna, pasâhna, nata thlonazy he a tao thei. Cha  dâh eihrâhpazy 
la thei nawpa cha dâh app sâkha a châ thei nawpa a châ.

This is where the Privileged Extension comes in - being a separate app
and much smaller, it can be installed as a system app and communicate
with the main app via AIDL IPC.

He he a phahnaina hluhpi a hnei:

* Khaw kao liata disk hmâna chyh viapa
* Khaw pasâhna cha F-Droid thy vei
* Thari tawhta dâh lâ patyhpa cha thlalôh â chhih via

He taona vyuhpa ta, hmâhtuh hluh chaipazy cha
https://f-droid.org/packages/org.fdroid.fdroid.privileged.ota ama tahpa
"Over-The-Air" (OTA) update ZIP file he ama patyh khoh via aw. He he OTA ZIP hmâpa ta
patyhpa a châ tita Privileged Extension he hmâpa ta pasâh nawpa a châ.
